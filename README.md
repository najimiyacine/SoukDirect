SoukDirect 🌱🚚

Farm Smart. Ship Together.

SoukDirect is a Souss-Massa-based Agri-Tech startup project developing a digital solution to improve connections between small-scale farmers, distributors, transport operators and professional buyers.

Our objective is to explore smarter ways of coordinating agricultural supply and demand, particularly through the consolidation of small shipments of fresh fruits and vegetables.

About the Project

Agricultural producers do not always have access to efficient and affordable logistics solutions for transporting their products to potential buyers. Fragmented supply, small shipment volumes and coordination difficulties can create additional costs and logistical inefficiencies.

SoukDirect aims to address these challenges by developing a digital platform that facilitates collaboration between agricultural stakeholders.

The initial project concept focuses on connecting producers in the Souss-Massa region with distributors and professional buyers in Marrakech, starting with fresh agricultural products.

Our Concept

SoukDirect is built around the idea of Smart Load Pooling: intelligently consolidating compatible agricultural shipments from multiple producers into shared transport routes.

The proposed solution aims to:

- Connect small-scale farmers with distributors and potential buyers.
- Consolidate smaller agricultural shipments into optimized loads.
- Facilitate coordination between producers, buyers and transport operators.
- Improve the utilization of existing transportation capacity.
- Help reduce avoidable logistics costs and delivery inefficiencies.
- Take product handling, freshness and delivery time constraints into account.

Rather than building and operating its own fleet from the outset, SoukDirect intends to explore a digital coordination model that makes better use of existing transport resources.

Initial Market Focus

The first proposed market segment is fresh fruits and vegetables.

Initial products under consideration include:

- Tomatoes
- Courgettes
- Bell peppers
- Other compatible seasonal agricultural products.

The initial geographical focus is the Souss-Massa–Marrakech corridor. The precise collection areas, shipment volumes and operational arrangements remain subject to field research and validation.

The Digital Solution

The envisioned platform will progressively incorporate features such as:

1. Shipment Registration: Producers submit product details, quantities, collection points and availability.
2. Smart Matching: The system identifies compatible shipments according to location, destination, timing and product requirements.
3. Load Consolidation: Compatible shipments are grouped to improve transport utilization.
4. Route Coordination: Transport routes and delivery sequences are organized according to operational constraints.
5. Shipment Tracking: Delivery progress and proof of receipt can be recorded.

These features describe the intended direction of the project. They are not presented as fully developed or operational functionalities.

Development Stage

SoukDirect is currently at the concept development and market-validation stage.

The next steps include:

- Conducting interviews with farmers, distributors and transport operators.
- Identifying suitable pilot products and collection areas.
- Assessing shipment volumes and existing logistics practices.
- Testing the feasibility of shared agricultural transport.
- Developing an initial digital prototype.
- Evaluating the economic and operational impact of the proposed solution.

Our approach is to validate the real needs of agricultural stakeholders before investing in a fully developed platform.

Our Vision

To contribute to a more connected, efficient and accessible agricultural supply chain by making shared logistics easier for small producers and professional buyers.
